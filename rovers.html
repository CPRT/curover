We improve on past designs every year. These are our rovers back to 2015, none of which could exist without the constant work of our team.

SPIKE

  • Lighter redesigned frame
  • 4 TPU wheels and rocker suspension
  • Comms system that streams 1080p video up to 1 km away
  • 6 degree-of-freedom arm with full inverse kinematics
  • RTK GPS accurate within 20 cm

Active

DUG

  • 6 custom 3D-printed TPU wheels that deform elastically to 50% of their diameter
  • 6-DOF arm with differential joint and a 10 kg carrying capacity
  • In-house PDB PCB managing 22.2 V and 100 A
  • 3D LIDAR and stereo camera for autonomous navigation
  • RTK GPS accurate within 20 cm, 2.4 GHz comms
  • Top speed 1.5 m/s

Retired

SAMI

  • Better comms and easier driving
  • Onboard spectrometer
  • Precise GPS
  • New arm and end effector

Retired

Waldo

  • Made it to URC
  • Lighter than Sparky
  • Fastest rover at CIRC, top speed 4 m/s
  • Same arm as Sparky with better control
  • Can still do push-ups

Retired

Sparky

Our second rover, designed from September 2016 to June 2017. It placed 2nd at the 2017 Canadian International Rover Challenge.

  • Base weight: 48 kg
  • Power: 12V 60Ah LiPo
  • Comms: 802.11g
  • Top speed: 2.2 m/s

Retired

H3

Our first rover, built from September 2015 to July 2016 for the UK University Rover Challenge. Despite constant comms issues, the team finished 4th.

  • Base weight: 37.2 kg
  • Power: 12V 60Ah LiPo
  • Comms: 802.11g
  • Top speed: 1.6 m/s

Retired
