Our team is made up of students from a diverse range of programs, united by our shared passion. The work is split between five subteams, each crucial to the rover.

Mechanical

The Mechanical Team designs and builds the chassis, suspension, and robotic arm, and makes sure they can handle the terrain and tasks of a simulated Mars.

Electrical

The Electrical Team develops the power systems, wiring, and sensors that keep the rover running, whatever the conditions.

Software

The Software Team builds the autonomy, vision, and control systems that let the rover navigate and operate with minimal input.

Science

The Science Team studies the terrain the rover works in, analyzing soil and detecting resources while simulating real planetary missions.

Admin

The Business and Administration Team manages sponsorships, outreach and logistics so the team has the resources it needs to succeed.
