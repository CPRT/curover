About us

We are an interdisciplinary undergraduate design team that builds Mars rovers to inspire and develop the next generation of STEM leaders.

Mechanical

Chassis, suspension and robotic arm, built for the terrain and tasks of a simulated Mars.

Electrical

Power systems, wiring and sensors that keep the rover running in any condition.

Science

Soil analysis and resource detection, simulating real planetary missions.

Software

Autonomy, vision and control so the rover can navigate with minimal input.

Business

Sponsorships, outreach and logistics that give the team the resources to succeed.
